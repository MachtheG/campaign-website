Raison d'être

“reason for being”

Raison d'être is a French phrase that translates literally to “reason for being,” and it refers to the most fundamental purpose or justification for a person's existence.

“For it is God who works in you to will and to act in order to fulfill His good purpose.”

Philippians 2:13 (NIV)

“For God has not given us a spirit of fear and timidity, but of power, love, and self-discipline.”

2 Timothy 1:7 (NLT)

“Have I not commanded you? Be strong and courageous. Do not be afraid; do not be discouraged, for the Lord your God will be with you wherever you go.”

Joshua 1:9

ALAAAAAAA, how did I get here?

Hi, my name is Maureen (Nyaguthii Ndung'u) and I am an incredibly hopeful political aspirant running for Member of County Assembly (MCA), Karen Ward, in the 2027 general elections.

Typing that, let alone saying it out loud, evokes all sorts of thoughts and feelings within me… more so because time seems to be moving faster than usual (or at least in my mind and in my world it is). From the moment I started proclaiming my intention at the beginning of the year, almost every passing moment has served as a constant reminder that there's so much to be done with seemingly very little time to go.

My cautiously timed declaration to run for a political post — to those around me including my friends, some family members, and various acquaintances with whom I've engaged on occasion — has been influenced by how I've perceived the different sorts of responses and reactions to the expression of my aspirations. As expected, the feedback has been on a spectrum. It's been good for the most part (with clear elements of surprise: AH, OH, and LOL), and hopefully it continues to be moving forward; down to subtle chuckles about whether it's a phase I'm going through (little to no offense has been taken, although I'm working very hard on becoming semi-hardened, LQTM). When left to my own devices and assumptions, I suppose I can appreciate the varied feedback, as I'm willing to bet that even the person I was 10 years ago might have also found this seemingly random aspiration somewhat out of character, considering my laid-back personality.

However, in the same breath, I have to confess that this journey started perhaps more passively than I'm willing to admit — in 2016, when I noticed I would get deeply irritated by a certain elected high-ranking official. I perhaps didn't realize how that discontentment would influence me in the not-so-distant future (10 years later).

Fast forward to 2022, six years after I transitioned from my last role in formal employment as a recruitment consultant, I was (and still am) working as a personal trainer. As life was slowly getting back to normal post-COVID, I decided to reach out to my dad for advice about wanting/needing a side hustle, after which I went ahead to set up a cute little quirky hybrid cyber cafe that doubled as a thrift shop. It was conveniently located next to home and work (work being the gyms I would service my clients from). However, the most important criterion for me during that period in my life was that I solely operated from Karen for anything and everything I wanted in my life. And coincidentally or not, the timing of the opening and running of the shop happened to align with the timing of the last elections. I intend to revisit this topic in another blog, because in hindsight it's in this shop that I admittedly began to entertain the thought of wanting to step up to run for MCA of Karen.

Why me? And why now?

As someone who considers themselves a bona fide “Karener,” I admittedly am very emotionally invested in the continuous growth and development of the only place I've known to be “my home” — and yours.

Not only have I been a Karen resident for over 35 years, I am one who has:

  • Attended both primary school and university from within the ward.
  • Worked for various establishments located in the area, and run my own small business as well.
  • Attended and worshipped in different churches in the area over the years.
  • Made several friends and acquaintances through the various businesses my family and I have patronized over the years.

Ultimately, having grown up in the area for the majority of my life, I can confidently declare that I am very familiar with the numerous strengths and weaknesses our ward has. I have been blessed to have been present to watch the ward evolve and grow before my eyes in real time, and it is also for this very reason that I am able to discern a “gap” — which I view as an opportunity for someone like myself to step up and exert themselves to be of service and value to my community and society at large.

I still haven't found the exact words to explain where this desire came from and why it remained in my heart, but I do know for a fact it's tied to what I discovered (a little bit later than most in my life): that my Raison d'être, a.k.a. “reason for being,” is to use my God-given gifts and the experience gained through my professional and personal journey to serve those around me for the greater good, wherever it is I find myself. And in this season, my spirit has led me to seek a role in leadership, in a familiar environment although in a very unfamiliar scope of duty.

Here I am, in whatever version of me you know, past or present: Maureen Ndung'u, “the humble but confident” educated and principled girl/lady from KJA or JKUAT (or High School); the bodybuilding and art enthusiast; the free thinker and people person; the daughter, the sister, the parent and the Tata (IYKYK); the adaptable and resourceful employee and entrepreneur. I'm inviting you to bet on me through: collaboration on community projects; financial sponsorship and contributions; in-kind support with your time and skills; spreading the word far and wide; and engaging with me to let me know what you need from me as your future representative.

When good people exit the arena, the arena belongs exclusively to those who seek power for its own sake.

Why now?

We stand at a critical juncture in our nation's history. Across Kenya, citizens are awakening to the reality that the quality of their lives is directly tied to the quality of their leadership.

Many capable, educated, and well-meaning individuals shy away or easily withdraw from political engagement, concluding that the system is irredeemable. But this withdrawal is precisely what enables the perpetuation of poor governance. When good people exit the arena, the arena belongs exclusively to those who seek power for its own sake.

The Cost of Apathy

Every time a capable citizen declares themselves “above politics,” they cede ground to those who view public office as a personal enterprise. Apoliticism is not wisdom — it is surrender. The most counterintuitive truth of democratic governance is that change does not happen despite our participation; it happens because of it.

Karen Ward is a microcosm of both our nation's promise and its challenges. Home to over ten tertiary-level education institutions, including some of the country's most prestigious universities, the ward possesses an extraordinary concentration of intellectual capital.

The urgency of this moment cannot be overstated. We have a window of opportunity — narrow and precious — to demonstrate that a different kind of politics is possible. A politics rooted in policy, not patronage. In service, not self-interest. In community, not clan.

Your partnership in this campaign would be an investment in proving that this alternative is not merely aspirational but achievable.

Glossary

IYKYK
An acronym that stands for “if you know, you know.” It is commonly used on social media platforms.
Microcosm
From the Greek mikros kosmos, meaning “little world” — a small-scale place, community, or situation that perfectly mirrors the characteristics, features, or dynamics of a much larger entity.
