Facts About Karen Ward

Discover the Largest Ward in Langata Constituency

📍 Location & Geography

Karen is the largest ward by area in Langata Constituency.

  • Constituency Area: Approximately 232 km²
  • Wards in Langata: South C, Nairobi West, Mugumoini, Highrise, and Karen
  • Known for expansive green spaces, tree-lined roads, and suburban character

🌳 Natural Beauty

Natural Beauty

Home to parts of Ngong Forest, providing unique access to nature within Nairobi. Indigenous trees and wildlife corridors connect to Nairobi National Park.

🏛️ Historical

Hover to explore →

Historical Significance

Named after Karen Blixen, Danish author of "Out of Africa." The Karen Blixen Museum attracts 100,000+ visitors annually.

🏪 Economic Hub

Hover to explore →

Economic Hub

Home to shopping centres, restaurants, hotels, and the famous Karen Market — a hub for 200+ local artisans and vendors.
